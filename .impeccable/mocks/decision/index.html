Budget: pick a direction

The same app, features and flows in three new looks. Each phone shows the two moments that matter most to you: the glance (Home) and the quick log (Add). The numbers are the ones from your October screenshot, used as sample data. Whichever you pick, the themes stay, redrawn inside that look, and both the Mac/Windows window and the iPhone app change.

DIRECTION 1 · MY RECOMMENDATION

Línea

Madrid's Metro signage, the system you read every day. The month is a metro line: today is the "you are here" stop, so you see at a glance how far through the month you are compared with how much you've spent. Categories get line-style roundels, and each theme is named after a line: Línea 10 navy, Línea 1 sky blue, Línea 2 red, Línea 5 green, and Noche for dark.

Feels
Clear, confident, local. White ground, one strong line colour, bold numbers.
Signature
The month line, with a stop per week, your position, and the pace.
Themes
Six, one per metro line colour, plus Noche (dark).
Risk
Metro line colours on categories need care so two similar ones never sit side by side.

DIRECTION 2

Taco

The tear-off desk calendar (calendario de taco): one big sheet a day. Home leads with today: the date as the calendar shows it, and today's allowance in calendar red. The month's total is one line below. Logging an expense shows what today's sheet will say after it.

Feels
Everyday, warm, a little playful. A white sheet on a light stone ground, black and calendar red.
Signature
Today's sheet, with the stack of the month's remaining days under it, thinning as the month goes.
Themes
Paper and ink pairs (red, blue, green ink; a dark "night" pad).
Risk
It puts "today" first and the month second, a small change to the one-clear-number rule.

DIRECTION 3

Campo

Spanish identity design (Cruz Novillo's flat-colour marks for Correos and the Comunidad de Madrid): one flat field of colour and one number. The whole Home screen is the field, and its colour is the state: green on plan, amber when spending runs ahead, red when over. You know how you're doing before reading anything. Categories and recent expenses sit on a white card below.

Feels
Bold, very simple, unmistakable. The least on screen of the three.
Signature
The ground colour is the budget state; adding an expense happens on the field itself.
Themes
The field's colour family (green, blue, plum, ink); the state shifts its shade.
Risk
A screen that turns red is loud, and themes must never hide the state.
